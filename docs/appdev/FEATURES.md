Flutter app for Android, iOS and the web; Chrome and Windows are also debug targets.
Package `com.personal.jingyaopaint`, Dart package `jingyao_paint`, project folder `C:\AIProjects\JingyaoPaint`, which is the root of the git repo https://github.com/leonfighter1/JoyPainting (moved from leonfighter1/JingyaoPaint on 2026-10-25; the local folder kept its name). The internal names (package, bundle ID, `.jypaint`, repo, web path) kept "Jingyao" through the v2.1 rename so updates install over older versions.

## v6.2.0 (2026-10-28): a progress bar while a painting opens
- **Opening card (正在打开「…」 56%)**: opening a big painting from My Paintings, or reopening the last one at start, shows a card at the top of the screen (`OpeningProgressCard`, over every page, taps go through, read out by screen readers) with the painting's name, a bar and the percentage, until all of it is on the paper. Small paintings open in one frame with no card. Code: `PaintingSession.opening` (`OpeningProgress`), `open(id, name:)`, `paperProgress`; `ProgressCard` in `gallery/sample_progress_card.dart` (the sample card now uses it); `_onOpening` in `main.dart`; `S.openingPainting`. While one is opening, another tap in the gallery is ignored.
- **Reading in steps** (the first 40 % of the bar): saved text of 300,000 characters or more (`PaintingDoc.stepsFrom`, some 1,500 strokes) is parsed 200,000 characters at a time (a chunked `JsonDecoder`) and its strokes built 500 at a time, with a frame between each step (`PaintingDoc.decodeInSteps`, `PaintingStore.load(onProgress:)`); smaller ones decode at once, as before.
- **Painting on the paper in slices** (the rest): after a different painting is shown, the canvas's cache paints each layer's picture as many strokes as fit in 24 ms per frame (`LayeredCache.sliceBudget`), the lowest layer first, and reports how far it has got (`LayeredCache(sliced: true)`, `paintInSlices`, `StrokeLayerCache.paint(clock:, budget:)`). The result is the same picture as painting it at once. Only the canvas slices; the replay, the thumbnails and the exports paint whole, and zooming, moving and undo repaint as before. The 16,869-stroke 古风揽月 painting went on the paper in 14 frames of 38–75 ms (desktop test VM), where it was one frame of about 1.5 s.
- Verified by: `test/opening_test.dart` (reading in steps gives the same painting with rising progress; slices give the same picture across layers, a see-through layer, the eraser, the blender and multiply; the replay's cache never slices; a big painting shows the card with its name and percentage and has every stroke on the paper at the end; a small one shows no card).

## v5.0.0 (2026-10-28): layers
- **Layers (图层)**, advanced level: the tools drawer starts with a 图层 row (the active layer and how many there are); it opens the layers, top first. 新建图层 adds one above the active layer (10 at most) and makes it active; tap a layer to draw on it; drag its preview to reorder; each has show/hide and lock buttons and a menu to rename, merge down (its own opacity is dropped) or delete it (asking first if something is drawn on it; the last layer can't go); a slider sets the active layer's opacity (one undo per drag). Every layer change can be undone. Code: `lib/drawing/layers.dart` (`PaintingLayer`), `DrawingController` (layers, active layer, `strokesOn`, `pickable`, `canDrawOnActive`), `canvas/layer_panel.dart` (`LayersRow`, `LayerSheet`), `SkillLevel.layers`. Verified by: `test/layers_test.dart`.
- **How layers draw**: each shown layer is its own sheet, painted bottom first at its opacity, so an eraser only cuts into its own layer and the blender only mixes its own layer. Hidden layers aren't painted. On screen each layer has its own kept picture (`LayeredCache`), so drawing on one leaves the others alone (Chrome, 3,058 strokes: unchanged from 4.1). Exports, printing, thumbnails, the replay and the GIF all compose the layers (`paintStrokes(..., layers:)`, `renderPng/Rgba/Image(..., layers:)`). The color picker and the paint bucket read every shown layer together; a fill goes onto the active layer.
- **Drawing on the active layer**: new strokes, text, fills and pasted strokes go onto the active layer; the move tool, box select, select all and the move tool's "last stroke" only take strokes from it, and only while it is shown and unlocked (switching layers drops the rest of a selection). On a hidden or locked layer, the brushes, text and the bucket draw nothing and a message says why (the touch doesn't move the paper either).
- **Saved**: strokes carry `layer` (none for the base layer) and paintings `layers` (only when there is more than the base layer), so one-layer paintings save exactly as before. Strokes are kept in layer order, so older versions show them stacked correctly, but ignore hidden layers, opacity and locks, and an eraser on an upper layer cuts through the layers under it there.

## v4.1.0 (2026-10-28): paint bucket, color picker, smooth big paintings, the drawing screen split up
- **Paint bucket (油漆桶, `BrushType.bucket`)**: every level, after the stamps. A tap fills the area of about the tapped color (tolerance 32) around it, closing gaps of about twice `AppSettings.fillGap` points (default 2; 补缝隙 slider 0–6 from intermediate), reaching one pixel under the edges; holes stay (specks under 6 px² are filled over). The fill is a free-shape stroke with an exact `Stroke.area` (outlines, even-odd), saved as `area` in paintings (older versions fill around the outer outline, smoothed, without holes); it can be moved, recolored, erased and undone. It uses the color, opacity, gradient and light & shadow (as each level allows); no size slider. Worked out in a background isolate (`compute`) on the apps; in browsers at most 1024 px across (96 ms on a 3,058-stroke painting). A thin progress bar shows while it works. Code: `lib/drawing/render/flood_fill.dart`, `canvas/tools/fill_tool.dart`, `PaperSampler`/`paperPixels` in `render/pixel_sampler.dart`. Verified by: `test/fill_test.dart`.
- **Color picker (吸管, `BrushType.picker`)**: intermediate and up, after the move tool. Touching the paper shows a loupe 84 px above the finger (below it near the top) with the 9×9 paper points under it magnified and ringed with their middle color; letting go sets that color (also added to the recent colors) and switches back to the brush used before. It reads what's seen (paper, blends, textures, see-through paint), from the kept picture when there is one. Alt + click picks with any tool; the I key selects it; the shortcut list includes both. Code: `canvas/tools/picker_tool.dart`, `canvas/picker_loupe.dart`. Verified by: `test/picker_test.dart`.
- **Smooth big paintings**: `StrokeLayerCache` (`render/layer_cache.dart`) keeps the finished strokes' layer (without the paper) as one picture at the screen's resolution for the current zoom step (1×, 2×, 4×, 8×); new strokes are painted onto it, the eraser over it, and up to 8 earlier pictures (about 64 MB together) are kept so undo and redo find theirs.
